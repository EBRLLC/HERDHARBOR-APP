# v1.8.4 Subscription / Payment-State Reliability Acceptance

This phase validates entitlement transitions without changing pricing, signup policy, plan visibility, trial policy, or stored herd data.

Required acceptance:
- Member checkout remains server-authoritative and idempotent
- Customer Portal remains available through the trusted billing backend
- webhook-synchronized entitlement is authoritative over stale browser state
- cancel/reactivate and paid-to-fallback transitions preserve records
- adult paid access falls to Free Adult where current policy requires; Junior remains distinct
- Owner/Admin/Founder/manual override protections remain intact
- trial authority remains backend-owned
- failed renewals remain recoverable without temporary destructive downgrade
- delayed webhook/browser refresh cannot own or deadlock application startup
- referral qualification and Member-month credit rules remain idempotent/audited
- access-tier changes never delete stored herd records
- Stripe secret and webhook signing credentials remain server-side

Any production billing acceptance requiring Stripe or webhook actions remains operator-controlled and external to browser startup.
