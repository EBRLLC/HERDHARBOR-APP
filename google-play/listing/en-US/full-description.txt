HerdHarbor is a farm management app for breeders, homesteaders, and small farms, with specialized rabbit-management workflows.

Keep animal profiles, identification, status, photos, weights, health notes, and pedigree information together. Record breeding dates, expected births, litter outcomes, offspring, recurring tasks, production, hay, expenses, income, customers, reservations, payments, and animal transfers.

HerdHarbor includes:

- Animal records for multiple livestock species
- Guided pedigrees with mobile viewing and printing
- Breeding, pregnancy, birth, litter, and offspring records
- Health, weight, task, and recurring-reminder tracking
- Production and hay records
- Monthly and full-year budgets
- Customer, reservation, sale, payment, and transfer records
- Bills of sale, invoices, receipts, QR cards, and spreadsheet exports
- Protected account sign-in and cloud synchronization
- Offline access, recovery snapshots, JSON backups, and restore tools
- Light and dark themes with phone and tablet layouts

HerdHarbor is a production farm management app with specialized rabbit-management workflows. Your HerdHarbor account and cloud records stay connected across supported web and Android experiences.
