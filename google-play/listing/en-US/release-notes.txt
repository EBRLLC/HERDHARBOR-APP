Alpha v1.4.0 introduces HerdHarbor Breeding Intelligence. Rabbit breeders can create optional genetic profiles, compare a buck and doe with Pair Analysis, review pedigree and previous-offspring evidence, see possible offspring color families, and save prediction history. Exact percentages are only shown when supported genotypes are fully known. Existing farm, animal, pedigree, breeding, health, sales, task, production, and cloud-sync records remain compatible.
