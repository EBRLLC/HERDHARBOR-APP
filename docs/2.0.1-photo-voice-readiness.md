# 2.0.1 Photo and Voice assisted-entry acceptance notes

## Photo entry
- Existing `record-photo-extract` remains the sole authenticated provider route for record-photo AI. Its server-side A1 authorization, per-user/global image quotas, data minimization and kill switch apply unchanged.
- `photo-assisted-entry-v1.8.3.js` continues to create only an editable draft. The canonical Animal or Health form is the sole save owner.
- An extracted registration document with **no visible species** is no longer silently assigned to Rabbit. The reviewer must explicitly record the species. This prevents a Golden Retriever document from silently becoming a rabbit record.
- For health/weight/medication photo drafts, ambiguous or missing animal matches continue to require explicit selection. Dosages are transcription drafts, not veterinary prescribing or medication instructions.
- A non-eligible account or provider failure does not affect manual Animal or Health forms. No photos or extracted farm fields are added to aggregate telemetry.

## Voice entry
- Voice is browser-local speech recognition/text parsing plus a reviewed canonical-form handoff, not an OpenAI API-backed voice service. Do not describe it otherwise.
- Only weight, medication and existing supported breeding instructions are parsed. Canine breeding and mismatched-species pairs are rejected; no whelping/estrus workflow is invented.
- The reviewed animal ID must refer to an existing animal, and the date must be in ISO form. Missing/ambiguous identity and invalid dates cannot reach canonical forms.
- User may cancel before the handoff, and no voice transcript is written to canonical animal or health records or monitoring logs.

## Public access
The Member/Founder/Junior access matrix remains unapproved and public AI switches remain off. This PR adds no AI activation, Supabase deployment, pricing, user account configuration, or production data edits.

R1 should still verify real photo and voice UX (including iPhone capture, cancellation and account switching) with owner-authorized test accounts. No screenshots/live runs are claimed here.
