## What v1.6.7 closes in code

1. A single current build identity (`1.6.7-alpha-completion-debt-1`) across build/PWA/manifest/Android/TWA/release metadata.
2. Current monitoring metadata (`HerdHarbor@1.6.7`, build `completion-debt-1`) instead of stale membership-review labels.
3. Market contribution automatic retry/backoff and post-Cloud-Sync retry.
4. Explicit Market opt-out deletion of prior contributed facts.
5. Stronger Market privacy for exact extrema: averages/medians at >=5; minimum/maximum at >=10 by default.
6. Production-only Sentry delivery acceptance tooling.
7. Production-only membership authorization acceptance tooling.
8. A manual, protected production workflow for Market deployment and acceptance; never automatic on merge/push.
9. A current release audit and dedicated completion contract.
10. Permanent documentation of what is complete, what was absorbed, and what is intentionally future.

## What v1.6.7 does not call complete

### Paid billing

The current billing feature flag remains `false`. A real paid-subscription launch requires store/provider products, credentials, purchase flow, receipt/provider verification, restore purchases, cancellation/renewal reconciliation, store review, and explicit activation. That work was not an accepted obligation of the v1.5.1 foundation and is not silently invented here.

### Full ARBA Standards & Judging

The repository contains an optional standards/reference foundation and multi-species genetics architecture. It does not contain or claim the complete licensed/copyright-controlled ARBA Standard of Perfection. A future dedicated release must handle edition/versioning, breed/class/fault/DQ content, licensing, historical retention, show-entry linkage, and correction states under its own acceptance contract.

### Business / marketplace / v2.0

HerdHarbor Business, Business Reporting & Operations, broader Sales/Marketplace functionality, and v2.0 Full Release remain future roadmap items.

## Definition of live

For this project, "live" means the release code is merged into the production release branch and required production deployment/acceptance steps for external services have completed. A draft PR, green unit tests, SQL file, Edge Function source file, unsigned AAB, or disabled provider adapter is not by itself a live service.
